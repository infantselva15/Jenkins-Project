Add a new record on Custom Setting "ConfigurazioneProvaSkyTryAndBuy"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
--------------------------- MANUAL PROCEDURE STEPS ----------------------------
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Go to setup 
2. In the quick find box, search for Custom Settings
3. Search and click on ConfigurazioneProvaSkyTryAndBuy
4. Click on "Manage" button
5. Click on "New" button
6. Insert:
	Name: BlockMaterialiResi
	msgError : Riconversione non eseguibile, materiali non in possesso del cliente
7. Click on Save
