Modify a record of the "ConfigurazioneProvaSkyTryAndBuy" Custom Setting
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
--------------------------- MANUAL PROCEDURE STEPS ----------------------------
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Go to setup 
2. In the quick find box, search for Custom Settings
3. Search and click on ConfigurazioneProvaSkyTryAndBuy
4. Click on "Manage" button
5. Click on "BlockMaterialiResi"
6. Click on Edit button 	
7. Flag MessageForOS field
8. Click on Save
