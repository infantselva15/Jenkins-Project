--------------------------- MANUAL PROCEDURE STEPS ----------------------------
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Below are the manual procedure to add "BlueBird_Archivio_WorkOrder" Visualforce page to the "Profile Partner Community Società Recupero" profile
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go To Setup-> Quick Find Search and type "Profile".
2. click on 'Profile'.
3. Open "Partner Community Società Recupero Crediti" profile.
4. In the App section select "Visualforce Page Access"
5. Click Edit
6. Select the checkbox of "BlueBird_Archivio_WorkOrder"
7. Click save
